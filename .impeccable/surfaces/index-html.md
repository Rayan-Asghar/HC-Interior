## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a zebra blind: alternating sheer and opaque bands slide with scroll, aligning to reveal one hero product at a time. It refuses the hero-plus-card-grid product template.
OWN-WORLD: Warm charcoal (#2b2927) fabric bands, bronze (#8c6d46) thread and rule lines, warm white (#f3efe8) sheer; per-chapter accent tints. Condensed display type echoing the logo wordmark, tracked small caps labels, product codes like HC-FL-014 as catalogue marks.
STORY: Visitor understands HC Interior is a 2005-established interiors supplier spanning homes to operating theatres, sees six hero products revealed through the blind, then browses sliding collections and enquires.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed canvas of horizontal blind bands, logo skyline faintly lit behind sheer bands; huge condensed "DESIGN BEYOND ORDINARY" lower-left, bronze; top bar with logo, Collections, Enquiry (count); primary CTA "Request a quote" bottom-right with scroll cue.
FORM: Zebra blind canvas scroll film (candidate 7, assigned), seed key 1783af4b. Catalogue grammar from Northern Cult (codes, quick view), collection naming from cabinets.com, editorial calm from Gensler.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
