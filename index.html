Do you supply only, or supply and fit?

Both. We can supply products on their own, or supply and install them. Tell us which you need when you enquire.

Can you source something that is not in the catalogue?

Yes. We keep selected products in stock and also source and import to your brand and project requirements.

Do you work on hospitals and clinics?

Yes. Our healthcare range covers modular operation theatres, hermetic doors, ceilings, epoxy and vinyl floors, handrails, bumper guards and medical panels.

Do you work on offices as well as homes?

Yes. Offices are served with partitions, sound proofing, SPC and carpet-tile floors, raised floors and ceilings.

How do I get a quote?

Add products to your enquiry list and send it on WhatsApp, or contact us with the room and what you have in mind. We will bring samples.
